Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

CyberBrief ("we") runs an internal publishing tool that uploads videos to the CyberBrief YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what that tool does with Google and TikTok data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the CyberBrief channel owner to publish to their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

Data we access

  • YouTube: the youtube.upload permission, used only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status on the CyberBrief channel. We do not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or account details.
  • TikTok: permission to upload videos to the CyberBrief account as drafts or posts.

How data is stored

The only data kept is an OAuth refresh token, stored encrypted in GitHub Actions secrets and used only by the upload job. Video files and their metadata are stored temporarily in the build environment and deleted automatically within 14 days.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google or TikTok user data to anyone. Data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services

CyberBrief's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions.
